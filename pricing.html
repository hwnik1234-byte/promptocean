Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. You will retain Pro access until the end of your current billing cycle.
Can I purchase just a single prompt?
Yes! If you don't want to subscribe to the Pro plan, you can purchase any premium prompt individually for a one-time fee directly from the prompt card.
How do I get paid for selling prompts?
Once you become a creator and your prompts are approved, you earn an 80% commission on every individual sale. Payments are routed directly to your connected Razorpay account.
